DATA HANDLING & EXTERNAL AI

Privacy & Security Overview

See what leaves the application, what is minimized, and where the prototype still needs human care.

Privacy gates enabled by default
Demo mode: synthetic/public sample data onlyDo not upload real patient records to this educational prototype. This screen explains the controls; it is not a certification or guarantee of compliance.
01 · TEXT PROMPTS

Minimize before AI

Patient name, reference, contact phone and facility address are not inserted into the Gemini prompt. Free-text symptom/report content is checked for the exact intake name, phone numbers, email addresses, Aadhaar/PAN-like strings and labelled identity fields.

Pattern-based minimization can miss identifiers. Demo mode also blocks several known direct-identifier patterns.
02 · REPORTS & IMAGES

Separate file consent

Original PDF/image bytes are blocked from Gemini until separate consent is provided. After opt-in, OCR inspects every page and masks likely identifier lines; only a flattened redacted derivative is sent if inspection and local report-name matching succeed. The original file is not used as the AI attachment.

OCR-assisted pixel masking reduces exposure but can miss identifiers. A failed or unreadable redaction pass blocks transmission. Use only approved synthetic/public samples.
03 · VOICE

Separate audio consent

The AI transcription action requires a separate explicit opt-in before raw recorded audio is sent to Gemini. Browser speech recognition may be used without invoking the AI transcription endpoint.

Raw audio is not automatically anonymized. Do not use real patient voices in demo mode.
04 · HUMAN REVIEW

Keep people in control

AI output remains advisory. Authorized reviewers must verify source evidence, resolve missing information, and make clinical/workflow decisions.

Use the configured database, secret management, HTTPS, access controls and retention policy for the deployment environment.
